## Why this batch

36 sell rules have been tested (Q200, Q201, Q221, Q231-Q243) and none beat holding. Every one of them was tested on
a rebound or slow-value strategy (gap_rebound, buyback_value, stock_screen), where selling weakness sells the
rebound. Professional trailing exits and volatility stops are built for trend trades (stop rules only help when
returns continue: Kaminski and Lo 2014). The demo runs two trend strategies, vol_squeeze and breakout_momentum,
whose entries and exits were never backtested ("no tested edge: forward test"). Their exits are fixed percentages
and short time limits. This batch tests the professional alternatives on those two, and whether the two entry
rules have any edge at all.

## Data

- Universe: the 451 US stocks of the Q183 universe (file names in research-data/tiingo_daily, SPY excluded).
- Prices: Yahoo daily OHLCV, dividend- and split-adjusted, 1995-01-03 to 2022-12-30, fetched 13 Nov 2026 with
  research/always_on/yahoo_fetch.py into research-data/yahoo_ohlc_1995 (honest User-Agent, owner approved Yahoo
  11 Nov). Nothing after 2022-12-31 is downloaded. SPY from the same source; VIX from research-data/fred/VIXCLS.csv.
- Bar cleaning (fixed now): rows without a close or with a price <= 0 are dropped; high is raised to
  max(open, close, high) and low lowered to min(open, close, low).
- Liquidity (loop rule, both strategies): median of adj close x volume over the 20 sessions up to the signal day
  >= $10m. Deviations from the live rules, stated now: the live $50m average dollar volume and $10 price floor of
  vol_squeeze are replaced by this rule (old adjusted prices are not comparable with a $10 floor); the live rules
  trade a 15-name watchlist, the test uses the whole liquid universe; no earnings block and no Wikipedia attention
  score (no free history before 2013); breakout_momentum buys at the trigger day's close (live buys intraday when
  the +5% move is seen).
- Bias: the universe is survivors (companies large in 2013-2026). Every return is measured against the equal-weight
  return of the same liquid universe on the same day, which removes the survivors' average drift, and the gate's
  stock-panel survivorship bound (1.0 point a year on the confirmation window) is applied to every config.

## The live rules, as tested (the baseline of every comparison)

Momentum off-switch (live, both): no new entry when, on the signal day, SPY's close is below the mean of its last
10 month-end closes, or the VIX last closed at 25 or more.

**vol_squeeze** (agent/market_agent/swing.py `squeeze`, called on bars up to signal day k): bandwidth percentile
<= 10% of the previous 250 sessions, close above the highest high of the prior 20 sessions, volume >= 1.5x the prior
20-session mean, close above the 200-session mean. Buy at the open of k+1 unless that open is more than 3% above
the close of k. Initial stop S0 = the higher of the 20-session low (through k) and 8% below the entry price; no
trade if S0 >= entry price. Exits, each session t from the entry day on:
1. time: at the open of the first session dated >= entry date + 28 calendar days;
2. stop: at the open if open <= S0, else at S0 if low <= S0 (on the entry day only the second part);
3. trailing: once the highest high from the entry day to t-1 is >= 1.10 x entry, at L = lowest low of sessions
   t-10..t-1: at the open if open < L, else at L if low < L. If 2 and 3 both trigger intraday, the higher level.

**breakout_momentum** (config active.strategies.breakout_momentum + review): close of k is >= 5% above the close of
k-1, and the multi-horizon review composite (agent/market_agent/review.py `score(factors(...))` on the last 252
closes and volumes through k, no attention input) is >= 0.25. Buy at the close of k. Exits, each session t > k:
1. time: at the open of the first session dated > entry date + 5 calendar days;
2. stop: at the open if open <= 0.95 x entry, else at 0.95 x entry if low <= it;
3. target: at the open if open >= 1.10 x entry, else at 1.10 x entry if high >= it (the stop is checked first);
4. review: at the close of t if the composite through t is <= -0.35.

**Sleeve accounting (every arm):** each strategy and each arm runs its own sleeve of 10 equal slots (10% of the
sleeve each). A signal is taken only if a slot is free after the exits up to that moment and the name is not
already held in that sleeve. More signals than slots: vol_squeeze takes the lowest bandwidth percentile first,
breakout_momentum the biggest day move first, ties by ticker. An empty slot earns the benchmark (zero excess).
Daily sleeve excess = sum over open trades of 0.10 x (trade return that day - equal-weight liquid-universe return
that day; open-to-close for both on a day entered at the open), minus costs. Monthly excess = sum of the days.
Costs: 0.15% of the trade a side (the T212 FX fee; Q224 measured 0.16%). costs_x2 = 0.30% a side. lag1 = every
entry one session later (vol_squeeze at the open of k+2 with the 3% chase rule on that open, breakout_momentum at
the open of k+1); exits unchanged.

## Batches and configurations (6 trials, N = 493 to 498)

Windows for all three: discovery 1996-07..2009-12 (162 months), confirmation 2010-01..2022-12 (156 months),
data = "panel".

**Batch Q251: let winners run (trailing exit instead of time limit and target), k = 2.** Series = alternative
sleeve minus live sleeve.
- Q251a (N=493) vol_squeeze: same entries and S0; no 28-day limit; the trailing exit (lowest low of the previous 10
  sessions) is active from the first session after entry, no +10% arming; forced exit at the open of the 251st
  session.
- Q251b (N=494) breakout_momentum: same entries; the 5% stop stays; no target, no 5-day limit, no review exit;
  trailing exit at the lowest low of the previous 10 sessions from the first session after entry; forced exit at
  the open of the 251st session.

**Batch Q252: volatility-scaled stop instead of a fixed-percentage stop, k = 2.** Series = alternative minus live.
- Q252a (N=495) vol_squeeze: S0 = entry - 3 x ATR20, where ATR20 is the mean true range of sessions k-19..k.
  Everything else as live.
- Q252b (N=496) breakout_momentum: stop = entry - 3 x ATR20 instead of 5%. Everything else as live.

**Batch Q253: do the two live rules have any edge, k = 2.** Series = the live sleeve's excess over the
equal-weight liquid universe.
- Q253a (N=497) vol_squeeze as live. Q253b (N=498) breakout_momentum as live.

Power (written before data; the edge and tracking error of these series are guesses, nothing comparable has been
measured): gate v3 power: 3.0%/yr at 6% TE, 162+156 months, k=2: P(pass) 32% (discovery 58%, confirmation 56%); a
no-edge config passes 0.25%: RUN. Honest note: at 2.0%/yr the same check says REDESIGN (P 11%), so each batch has
a fair chance only if the effect is at the top of a plausible range. The 2012-2022 Tiingo panel alone said
REDESIGN at every size (P 4% to 14%), which is why the history was extended to 1995 with Yahoo.

## What each outcome does (fixed now)

- Q251 or Q252 config PASSES gate v3: the alternative exit replaces the live exit of that strategy in the demo
  agent, labelled a gate-v3 pass on a survivor panel (forward test, kill rule unchanged).
- Q251 or Q252 config FAILS but its discovery-window mean is positive with t >= 1.0, and positive on the discovery
  window with costs x2 and with lag1: BENCH. The live exit was never tested either, so the better-measured of two
  unproven rules becomes the demo rule, labelled unproven. The confirmation window is not used for this decision
  (the gate never reads it for a config stage 1 dropped; its mean is printed for information only).
- Otherwise: FAIL, the live exit stays, and the result is recorded on the rules page.
- Q253 config PASSES: the strategy's evidence line becomes "gate-v3 pass (survivor panel)". FAILS with a negative
  discovery-window mean after costs: new buys of that strategy are paused in the demo (as drop_rebound was after
  Q223), open trades keep their exits. FAILS with a positive mean: stays a forward test, evidence line "tested and
  failed (Q253)".
- Also reported, not the gate: trades, win rate, mean trade return and holding days per arm, each third of the
  sample, DSR at N, sr_monthly, n_months, skew, kurt.

Prior: low to moderate. Momentum after large one-day moves and range breakouts in large US stocks is weak in the
published record after costs; if there is continuation, the trailing exits should show it.
