# Q274: peak-cycle guards for the pick-1 screener (owner question 20 Nov 2026: "are the 3 tests enough to screen a stock?"). Pre-registered 20 Nov 2026 ~13:10Z, before reading any guarded result

Motivating case: Sandisk (memory maker at a cyclical high). Pick-1 scores momentum, gross profitability and FCF yield, and all three look best at a cyclical peak.

**Gate v3 power (panel, 2015-2022 is all the history the guards allow, because SEC metrics start 2012 and the guards need 24+ months of the company's own history):** gate v3 power: 3.0%/yr at 5% TE, 48+48 months, k=1: P(pass) 15%: REDESIGN. Even generous 3%/yr over 72+72 months gives 25%, and those months don't exist for a 5-year guard. So this is NOT a gate-v3 edge trial. It is a pre-registered **risk-guard adoption check** with a no-harm rule, the same route that adopted Q214 tranches and the -30% safety stops. It is counted in the registry (counts_for_dsr) because data is read with a hypothesis.

Guards (applied to the pick-1 gated set before ranking; data = research-data monthly_metrics via pick1.load, exploration to 2022-12):
- **G1 margin vs own history:** TTM operating margin now <= 1.5x the company's median TTM operating margin over the previous 60 months (needs >= 24 months). Drops names earning far above their own norm.
- **G2 earnings stability:** TTM operating income > 0 at t-12 and t-24 as well as now, and the coefficient of variation of TTM operating income at (t, t-12, t-24) < 0.5.
- **G3 momentum cap:** drop names whose 12-1 momentum is above the 95th percentile of the screened universe that month (crash-risk proxy).
- **Config Q274a = base pick-1 + G1 + G2 + G3**, everything else identical (top 15, max 3 per SIC2, December rebalance, same costs). G1-G3 one at a time reported descriptively only.

Adoption rule (fixed now): adopt Q274a in the live screener if ALL hold over 2015-01..2022-12: (1) guarded minus base >= -0.5%/yr; (2) neither half (2015-18, 2019-22) worse than -1.0%/yr; (3) guarded worst 12-month return vs universe and max drawdown are no worse than base. Otherwise keep the base screen. Adoption is a risk control, not a claimed edge; it never goes to the go-live scoreboard.
Also reported: names the guards removed each year and their next-12-month return; whether the guards would drop SNDK/MU in the current live list.
