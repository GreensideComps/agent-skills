# Q272 gate-v3: US long-only JKP factor momentum (long legs of the factors that worked over the last 12 months), pre-registered 20 Nov 2026 ~04:20Z, before reading any return

Gate: research/GATE_V3.md (gate.py). Batch id Q272, data = none (index-style portfolios), k = 1. Follows the Q271 JKP design rule.
Data: research-data/jkp/jkp_usa_portfolios_monthly_vw_cap_to2022.csv.gz (all 153 factors, tercile pf 1-3, vw_cap, excess of T-bill, to 2022-12); direction from jkp_usa_all_factors (column `direction`). Developed ex-US file for the second confirmation.
- **Config Q272a:** each month t, for every factor with 12 months of history, signal = sum of (long leg - short leg) over months t-12..t-1 (long leg = pf 3 if direction = 1 else pf 1; short leg the other end). Hold an equal-weight mean of the LONG legs of the factors with a positive signal (need >= 10). Monthly excess = that composite - equal-weight mean of ALL three terciles of ALL factors available that month (same vw_cap weighting; Q271 rule, removes the capped-weight size tilt), minus 1.00%/yr cost (monthly reselection).
- Discovery 1963-07..1999-12 (438 months). Confirmation 2000-01..2022-12 (276 months).
- Stress: costs_x2 = 2.00%/yr. lag1 = signal over t-13..t-2 (one month stale), 1.00%/yr.
- Power: gate v3 power: 1.0%/yr at 3% TE, 438+276 months, k=1: P(pass) 37% (discovery 77%, confirmation 48%); a no-edge config passes 0.50%: RUN
- Forwarding rule (fixed now): a gate pass goes to the office as a forward test ONLY if (1) the same rule on developed ex-US (1991-01..2022-12, needs 12m warm-up from 1990) is positive after the 1.00%/yr cost, and (2) the timing-only spread (composite minus EW long legs of all factors, before cost) is positive in 2000-2022. Both descriptive, no extra configs.
- Also reported (not the gate): excess vs Mkt-RF, post-2007, DSR at current N, TE, skew, kurt, average number of factors held.
- Where the idea came from: factor momentum (Arnott-Clements-Kalesnik-Linnainmaa 2019/2023, Gupta-Kelly 2019, Ehsani-Linnainmaa 2022) is reported to survive out of sample and to explain stock momentum. Registry grep 20 Nov: no factor-momentum / factor-timing trial (Q269 was a static cluster composite). No JKP return has been read for this design beyond the Q269/Q271 static composites.
Honest prior: the papers find it in long-short factor returns; for long legs vs the all-terciles average the edge should be about half, ~1%/yr before costs, and the confirmation window overlaps the published samples (not clean). 1%/yr cost is a heavy haircut, so a FAIL is the most likely result. Use if it passes: labelled forward test only; implementation would need a stock-level build on our own panel.
