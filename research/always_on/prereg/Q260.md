## Rule (turn-of-month exposure timing at equal exposure-days)
- Calendar from the union of trading days. For each month end E (last session of the month), the TOM window is
  sessions E-1, E, E+1, E+2, E+3 (returns earned on those sessions; i.e. buy at close of E-2, sell at close of E+3).
- Monthly excess for calendar month m = sum of r_d over TOM sessions assigned to m (window keyed by the month of E)
  minus (n_TOM / n_other) x sum of r_d over the other sessions of the same span (the sessions after the previous
  window and up to E-2). So both legs carry the same market exposure-days; the excess is the TOM premium.
- Cost: one round trip per month on the TOM leg, 2 x 0.15% = 0.30% subtracted each month.

## Robustness: costs x2 (0.60%/month); lag1 = window shifted one session later (E..E+4).
