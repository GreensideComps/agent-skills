## Data
- Prices: `research-data/yahoo_ohlc_1995` (Q183 universe, adjusted daily OHLCV 1995-2022), loaded and cleaned with

- Benchmark: the equal-weight liquid universe per day, close-to-close and open-to-close, as in Q251.

## Q255 (short-term edge): post-filing gap continuation
- Event session k = first session with date strictly after the filing date.
- Signal: liquid at k-1; open[k] / close[k-1] - 1 >= +4%; close[k] >= (high[k] + low[k]) / 2.
- Trade: buy open k+1 (skip if open k+1 > 1.03 x close k), hold 20 sessions, sell close k+20. No stop.
- Sleeve: 10 slots, first come by (entry day, larger gap first, ticker); 0.15% a side; monthly excess vs the
  benchmark (Q251 `sleeve`).
- Config id: Q255. Discovery 1996-07..2009-12, confirmation 2010-01..2022-12 (same as Q251).
- Power: 2%/yr at 4% TE, 162+156 months, k=1: P(pass) 40% RUN.

## Q256 (sell rule): gap-low exit
- Same Q255 signals and entries. Exit at the first close below low[k] (the gap-day low), else close k+20.
- Config id: Q256 = Q256 sleeve minus Q255 sleeve (monthly).
- Power: 1%/yr at 2% TE, 162+156 months, k=1: P(pass) 40% RUN.
