## How much to trust the screen

The screen was back-tested on 600 companies from 2012 to 2025, using only data available at each date. It ranked
companies the right way more often than not: its top picks beat the rest in 11 of 14 years. It narrowly failed the
pre-set significance bar (t = 1.92 against 2.0). So this plan mostly manages risk, cutting weak and tiny
positions and concentration, and does not make big bets on the scores.
