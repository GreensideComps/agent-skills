## How to read this

- A t below 2 with an MDE above about 0.02 means the test could not have seen a typical-sized effect: 'not detected', not 'shown ineffective'.
- A significantly negative IC or alpha is evidence against the signal in the form tested.
- Longer horizons use overlapping outcomes; the non-overlapping t is the honest check when the two differ.
- These are exploration-period numbers. Any idea they suggest is a new hypothesis that must be registered and later confirmed on the holdout; it is not evidence on its own.
