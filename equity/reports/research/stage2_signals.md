## Pass rules (from STRATEGY_V2 2.2, frozen)

Pass needs all four: IC t >= 2; deflated Sharpe >= 0.9 across all trials; top quintile beats the universe after costs in at least 2 of 3 sub-periods; still positive at 3x costs. Fail: mean IC <= 0 or IC t < 1. Otherwise inconclusive. A pass is evidence tier E3 for that signal in large caps only; signals are not combined until each passes alone.
